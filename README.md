## What you can explore

Project M.A.T.E.R.N. brings maternal health information together in one place. It is designed for people planning a family, expectant mothers, new mothers, and those supporting them.

The site has ten guides: Prenatal Care, Childbirth, Postnatal Care, Nutrition & Lifestyle, Family Planning, Health Education, Emotional Support, Healthcare Provider Communication, Community Resources, and Record Keeping.

## Features

- **Find a topic easily.** Browse the topic cards, use the search box, or narrow the list by category.
- **Get a quick preview.** Hover over a card to see a short introduction, then select it to read the full guide.
- **Go straight to what matters.** Each guide has a contents menu that takes you to a selected section.
- **Read in familiar language.** Topic headings are in English, while the main guidance is in Filipino.
- **Use it wherever you are.** The layout adapts to phones, tablets, and computers, with a light or dark display option.
- **Explore freely.** No account or sign-in is needed.

## How to use the site

Select **Explore Topics** or **Find Your Guidance** on the homepage. Choose a topic card, then use the contents menu within the guide to jump to the information you need. You can return to the topic list whenever you want to explore another subject.

Project M.A.T.E.R.N. was developed by fourth-year nursing students of Trinity University of Asia as a Plan-Do-Study-Act project for Nursing Leadership and Management. The guides are educational resources; for advice about your own health, speak with a healthcare professional.

For people maintaining or publishing the website, see the [deployment guide](DEPLOYMENT.md).
